how this works

Read a little, then do a little

Each session follows an interactive rhythm to help you build enterprise intuition:

BlockWhat it means when you see it
// conceptPlain-language explanation with real-world analogies, architecture diagrams, and syntax breakdowns.
// try itAn interactive code simulation with a Run button to test inputs and see container responses live.
// terminalA simulated administrative shell. Execute commands like jboss-cli.bat, mvn clean package, or asadmin in a safe environment.
// check yourselfTargeted multiple-choice assessment questions with immediate feedback and detailed explanations.
// try it yourself (review)Consolidation labs every 2–4 sessions focusing on debugging, anti-patterns, and end-to-end integration.
✓
Works Offline, On Any Device Everything you read, every simulation and every quiz runs from a plain file with no internet connection at all. Two exceptions: the optional videos at the end of Sessions 1 & 2 need a connection, and the hands-on labs need WildFly and Maven installed — Session 1 walks you through that. Your progress (completed sessions, quiz scores, light/dark theme) is saved automatically in your browser's local storage.

Course Schedule & Syllabus

Structured into 5 weeks covering all 14 official sessions, 6 hands-on review labs, and prerequisite and bonus modules.

✓
What the little numbers mean Every page shows the unit it covers in the Presenter's Manual, so you can always match a page back to the printed course. TL17 is manual unit 17 — the sidebar shortens it to 17. Two pages cover two units at once and say so: 14+16 means the page covers unit 14 and unit 16, because the manual splits that material differently from the way this course teaches it. 11a and 11b are the two halves of unit 11. SS1 and SS2 are the self-study blocks, P21–P23 are the three practicals, and Bonus / Ref are extras that are not part of the assessed syllabus.
